- Repository: https://github.com/Zeejay0/gathered-scenes-zine-skill
- Focus: Transform photos into editorial zine / collage / handmade publication style artwork.
- Best for:
  - travel memories
  - lifestyle content
  - social media visuals

- Weakness: not intended for photorealistic commercial product shots.
